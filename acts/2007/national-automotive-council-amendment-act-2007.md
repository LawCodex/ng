# National Automotive Council (Amendment) Act, 2007

NATIONAL AUTOMOTIVE COUNCIL (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2007. EXPLANATORY

MEMORANDUM

This Act amends the National Automotive Council Act to abolish the two per cent of the Cost Insurance and Freight

(CIF) value of all imported Fully Built Units (FBU) auto component spare parts, Completely Knocked Down (CKD) and raw materials brought in for the automotive sub-sector and make provisions for alternate funding of the existing National Automotive Council. ,\,
