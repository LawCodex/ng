# National Minimum Wage (Amendment) Act 2000

National Minimum Wage (Amendment) Act 2000

National Minimum Wage (Amendment) Act 2000 2000 Act No 1

Laws of the Federation of Nigeria An Act to amend the National Minimum Wage Act to provide for a revised National Minimum Wage and for purposes connected therewith.

1st day of May 2000 Be it Enacted by the National Assembly of the Federal Republic of Nigeria as follows 1.

The National Minimum Wage Act (in this Act referred to as "the Principal Act") as amended, is hereby further amended as set out in this Act.

## Section 2

Section 1 of the Principal Act is amended in Sub-section (1) by substituting therefor the following new Sub-section, that is

(1) as from the commencement of this Act, it shall be the duty of every employer (except as provided for under this Act) to pay a wage not less than National Minimum Wage of N5,500.00 per month to every worker under his establishment.

## Section 3

This Act may be cited as the National Minimum Wage (Amendment) Act 2000.
