Summary

An Act to repeal the National. Assembly Service Commission Act Cap. 236 Laws of the Federation of Nigeria 1990, to provide for a new Commission to reflect the current structure of the National Assembly ; and for connected purposes.

of the Bill

This Bill seeks to repeal the National Assembly 14th Sept., Service Commission Act Cap. 236 Laws of the 2000 Federation of Nigeria 1990 and to provide for a new Commisison so as to bring it into conformity with the present structure of the National Assembly. The Bill gives power of nomination of the members of the Commission to the National Assembly but appointnient can only be made by the President of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. The Bill also gives the Commission the power to appointment, discipline, promotion and dismissal of staff of the National Assembly.
