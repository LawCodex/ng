# Pension Reform Act

A BILL FOR AN ACT TO FURTHER AMEND THE PENSION REFORM ACT CAP P4 LFN 2004 TO PROVIDE FOR DEFINITE PERCENTAGE A RETIREE CAN WITHDRAW FROM HIS RETIREMENT SAVINGS ACCOUNTED AND FOR OTHER MATTERS RELATED THERETO Sponsored by Senator Aliyu Magatakada Wamakko (Sokoto Central) ENACTED by the National Assembly of the Federal Republic of Nigeria: Amendment of Pension Reform Act CAP P.4 LFN 2004

• The Pension Reform Act CAP P.4 LFN 2004 (hereafter referred to as “the Principal Act”), is hereby amended as set out in this Bill.

• Amendment of Section 7 Section 7 of the Principal Act is amended in –

• Section 7(1) by inserting the words “or retires, disengages or is disengaged from employment” as provided under section 16 (2) (a) and (b) of this Act immediately after the phrase “whichever is later” in line two instead thereof:

• In 7 (1) (a) by inserting the words “of up to 75 percent” immediately after the words “a lump sum” instead thereof.

• In 7(2) by inserting the words “(C)” immediately after the words “16 (2)” in line two instead thereof.

EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM The Bill seeks to provide succor to retirees in the delay and other difficulties they are encountering in withdrawing their saving from retirement savings accounts.
