EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM

The Act provides for the issue out from the Consolidated Revenue Fund of the Federation a total sum of (N894,214,805,186.00) only out of which N397,856,858,542 only is for Recurrent •Expenditure, while the balance of N496,357 ,946,644 only is for contribution to the Development Fund for Capital Projects for the service of the Federation for the year ending 31st December, 2001. "Other Items" under Part B in the Schedule to the Act does not form part of the moneys issued out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund for the said period .

NationalAssembly Service Commission Bill, 2000

An Act to repeal the National. Assembly Service Commission Act Cap. 236 Laws of the Federation of Nigeria 1990, to provide for a new Commission to reflect the current structure of the National Assembly ; and for connected purposes.

This Bill seeks to repeal the National Assembly 14th Sept., Service Commission Act Cap. 236 Laws of the 2000 Federation of Nigeria 1990 and to provide for a new Commisison so as to bring it into conformity with the present structure of the National Assembly. The Bill gives power of nomination of the members of the Commission to the National Assembly but appointment can only be made by the President of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. The Bill also gives the Commission the power to appointment, discipline, promotion and dismissal of staff of the National Assembly.
