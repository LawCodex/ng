# Pension Reform (Amendment) Act, 2011

PENSION REFORM (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2011 EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM This Act amends the Pension Reform Act No.2 of 2004 in order to, among other things, exempt members of the Armed Forces of the Federation and members of the Intelligence and Secret Services from the application of the scheme so as to eliminate the problems associated with the contributory pensions scheme as it concerns the military personnel and members of the Intelligence and Secret Services in line with international best practices.
