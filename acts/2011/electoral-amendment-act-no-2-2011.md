# Electoral (Amendment) Act (No.2), 2011

This Act amends the Electoral Act, No.6, of 2010 as amended to abridge the time from 60 days to 30 days within which the Independent National Electoral Commission shall stop the registration of voters before any general election under the Act.

ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS Section:

## Section 1

Amendment of section 9(5) of the Principal Act.

## Section 2

Citation.

A Bill For

An Act to amend the Electoral Act, No. 6, 2010, as amended.

[ ] Commencement Enacted by the National Assembly of the Federal Republic of Nigeria:

1\. Section 9(5), line 2 of the Electoral Act No.6, 2010 Amendment oT (the Principal Act) is amended by substituting for the figure Principal cr . “60”, the figure “30”.

2\. This Act may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) Act Citation (No.2), 2011.
