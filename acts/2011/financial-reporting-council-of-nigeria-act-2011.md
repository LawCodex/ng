## SCHEDULE

FINANCIAL REPORTING COUNCIL OF NIGERIA ACT, 2011

A Bill For An Act to repeal the Nigerian Accounting Standards Board Act, No.22 of2003 and enact the Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria charged with the responsibility for, among other things, developing and publishing accounting and financial reporting standards to be observed in the preparation of financial statement of public entities in Nigeria; and for related matters.
