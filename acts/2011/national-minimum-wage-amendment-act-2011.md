# National Minimum Wage (Amendment) Act, 2011

EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM This Act further amends the National Minimum Wage Act, Cap. N61 Laws of the Federation of Nigeria, 2004 as amended, to provide for a revised national minimum wage and also to provide for a realistic penalty regime for violation of the provisions of the Act.
