The High Court of the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja, shall consist of(a) a Chief Judge ofthe High Court of the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja; and

## Section 2

The principle of "Federal Character of Nigeria" shall be applied in the appointment of judges of the Court provided that nothing in this Act shall affect or negate the proceeding of the Court by reason only that the 'number of judges of the Court remains at anytime below thirty-seven.

EXPLANATORY

MEMORANDUM

This Act prescribes the number of Judges of the High Court of the Federal Capital Territory, Ahuja to a maximum number of37 Judges as permitted by Section 255

(2) (b) of the 1999 Constitution. This Act also makes the application of the principle of "Federal Character" mandatory in the appointment of such Judges in compliance with Section 14 (3) of the 1999 Constitution.
