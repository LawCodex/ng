Summary of Contents of the Bill

The Corrupt Practices and other Related Offences Act, 2003

An Act to Prohibit and Prescribe Punislunent for Corrupt Practices and Other Related Offences and establish Anti-Corruption Commission.

The Act seeks to prohibit and prescribe punislunent for corrupt practices and other related offences. It further seeks to establish AntiCorruption Connnission vesting it with-the responsibility for investigation and recommendation for prosecution or otherwise to the Attorney-General of the Federation or of a State. It also makes provision of the protection of anybody who gives information to the Commission in respect of any offence connnitted or likely to be committed by any other person.
