EXPLANATORY

MEMORANDUM

This Act establishes the Small and Medium Scale Industries Development Agency to be charged with the responsibility for promoting and facilitating the development programmes in the Small and Medium Scale Industries sub-sectors. By establishing the Agency, the Act further enhances and supports services to accelerate rural development, poverty alleviation and eradication.
