# Fct High Court Number of Judges Amendment Act 2016

JUDGES) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2016 EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM

This Act amends the High Court of the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja (Number of Judges) Act No. 15, 2003 to increase the number of Judges from the current thirty-seven to seventy-five to provide for sufficient number of Judges in view of the increase in the number of cases in the Court's dockets.

For An Act to amend the High Court of the Federal Capital Territory, Ahuja, (Number of Judges) Act No. 15, 2003 to make provision for the increase of the number of Judges from thirty-seven to seventy-five; and for related matters Commencement

ENACTED by the National Assembly of the Federal Republic of Nigeria:

## Section 1

The High Court of the Federal Capital Territory, Ahuja, (Number of Judges) Act No. 15, 2003 (in this Act referred to as "the Principal Act") is amended as set out in this Act.

## Section 2

Section 1 (b) of the Principal Act is amended by substituting for the words, "thirtyseven", the words, "seventy-five".

## Section 3

Section 2 of the Principal Act is amended by deleting, after the word, "Court" in line 2, the words, "provided that nothing in this Act shall affect or negate the proceeding of the Court by reason only that the number of judges of the Court remains at time below thirty-five".

## Section 4

This Act may be cited as the High Court of the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja (Number of Judges) (Amendment) Act, 2016.
