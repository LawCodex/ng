FACILITY MANAGEMENT COUNCIL OF NIGERIA BILL, 2015 A Bill For

An act to establish the Facility Management Council of Nigeria (FMCN) charged with the responsibilities to among other things register persons and organisations seeking to practice facility management, set regulations and standards, inspect facilities, discipline erring members, sanction facilities, advance professionalism in the study and practice of facility management, organize expositions, publish books, journals, magazines, and other periodicals, as well as regulate and control the practice of facility management profession and related matters.

Sponsor: Sen. Ahmad Abubakar (Adamawa South) Co-sponsors: Sen. Ali Ndume and Sen. Abdul’aziz Nyako ( ) commencement

ENACTED by the National Assembly of the Federal Republic of Nigeria

• Establishment of the Facility Management Council of Nigeria

(1) There is established a body known as the Facility Management Council of Nigeria (in this Bill referred to as “the Council”).

(2) The Council –

(a) Shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal and may sue and be sued in its corporate name; and

(b) Acquire, hold, mortgage, purchase and seal deal with property, whether movable or immovable, real or personal.

## Section 2

Composition of the governing Council

(1) There is established for the Council a governing Council (in this bill referred to as “the Council”) which shall be charged with the responsibility for administration and general management of the Council.

(2) The Council shall consist of –

(a) A President who shall be the Chairman

(b) Deputy President;

(c) Immediate past President of the Council,

(d) 6 members representing the Nigerian Facility Management Association (NFMA) to be established by this Act

(e) Immediate Past President of Nigerian Facility Management Association

(f) Two persons who shall be members of the Council to represent institutions of higher learning in Nigeria offering courses leading to an approved facility management qualifications, in rotation, so however that the two shall not come from the same institution;

(g) One representative from any of the following Federal MDAs –

• Power, Works & Housing

• Transport/Aviation

• Environment

• Health

• Education

• Petroleum

• Industries

• Communication

• A representative of Infrastructure Concession Regulatory Commission;

(h) Facility Manager of the Federation

(3) The President, Deputy President shall each hold office for a term of two (2) years from date of their election; and the President shall be the Chairman at meetings of the Council where in the event of death, incapacity or inability for any reason of the President, the Deputy President shall act as President for the unexpired portion of the term in office as Chairman, as the case may be, pending the Annual General Meeting where election shall be conducted.

(4) Where the President or Deputy President ceases to be a member of the Council he shall cease to hold any of the offices designated under this clause.

(5) The Registrar shall be the Secretary to the Council.

(6) The Council may increase the membership of Council as it may deem necessary.

(7) The provision set out in the schedule to this bill shall have effect with respect to the qualifications and tenure of office of members of the Council and other matters.

## Section 3

Election of President and Deputy President

(1) The President and Deputy President shall-

• Be elected at the annual general meetings of the Council; and

• Hold office each for a term of two (2) years renewable for another term and no more

(2) A person shall be qualified for election to the office of President and Deputy President of the Council if he or she;

(a) Is a citizen of Nigeria:

(b) Has been educated up to at least University or Polytechnic level with a Post Graduate Degree, Professional Membership Certificate or their equivalent;

(c) Has paid all his or her dues for five consecutive years immediately preceding the year of election; and

(d) Has not been adjudged a lunatic or declared to be of unsound mind, adjudged or declared to be bankrupt;

## Section 4

Functions of the Council The functions of the Council shall be to-

(a) Advance art and science of facility management;

(b) Determine and view, from time to time the academic standards, knowledge and skills that shall be attained by persons seeking to qualify as registered members of the Facility Management Council of Nigeria;

(c) Ensure that its members maintain a reputable and high standard of behavior expected of any professional in facility management, facility consultancy, facility services in Nigeria;

(d) Provide for the training, education and conduct of examination of persons desiring to become professional facility managers according to the provisions of this bill whether in Nigeria;

(e) Conduct induction courses for aspiring members for a specified period of time as may be determined by regulations of the Council;

(f) Certification of private and public facilities managers to ensure their standards and compliance with relevant laws in the development and management in Nigeria irrespective of where he obtains the qualification;

(g) Issue license to any person qualified to practice the profession of facility management in Nigeria irrespective of where he obtains the qualification;

(h) Regulate the discipline and professional conduct of its members;

(i) Promote and protect the welfare of its members

(j) Provide learning and educational resources;

(k) Organize-

(i) Conferences, seminars, symposia, meetings, workshops and lectures on facility management and related issues

(ii) Post-qualification courses for its members; and

(l) Ensure Facility Management is embedded in infrastructure development and management both in the public and private sector with the setting up of Office of the Facility Manager Of the Federation as well as at all state levels - Office Of The Facility Manager;

(m) The Office of Facility Manager shall be responsible for defining, designing and implementing strategies for the management of all government buildings and infrastructures;

(n) Perform such functions as are incidental to its functions or as the Council may deem necessary for the attainment of all or any of its functions

## Section 5

Membership of the Council

(1) Subject to the provision of this bill, persons admitted to membership of the Council shall possess adequate interest, knowledge and understanding of facility management and related matters and must be registered as member in the category of -;

• Registered Facility Manager

• Corporate Member

(2) Person qualified and registered as member of the Council shall have the privileges of –

(a) Using the abbreviation as Registered Facility Manager;

(b) Affixing a member seal and stamp on every document endorsed or emanating from him or her.

(3) Subject to the provision of this clause, no other title or abbreviation shall be used to describe membership of the Council.

## Section 6

Procedures for Membership

(1) A person who is applying for membership shall, in addition to evidence of qualification, satisfy the Council that:-

• He or she is of good character; and

• Has not been convicted by any court or Tribunal in Nigeria or elsewhere of an offence involving fraud or dishonesty, or such other elsewhere of an offence as, in the opinion of the Board, would render the applicant unfit to be a member of the Council.

(2) The Registrar shall place before the Council all applications for admission stating in each case whether the applicant is qualified for registration in the class under which he or she seeks for admission and if not so qualified, whether he or she qualifies for admission under any other class.

(3) Where the Council is satisfied that the person is qualified for admission under the class for which he or she applies, and upon payment of the prescribed fees by the applicant, the registrar shall enroll the person-

• In that class, and issue him or her a certificate of membership appropriate for that class.

## Section 7

Duties of the Members

(1) It shall be the duty of a registered member of the Council to abide by the rules, regulations, code of conduct and any other policy made by the Council for the purpose of sound practice.

(2) A member of the Council shall conduct his or her behaviour in a manner to uphold the dignity and reputation of the Council.

## Section 8

Powers of the Council The Council shall have powers to do anything which in its opinion is calculated to facilitate the carrying on of the activities of the Council.

## Section 9

Appointment of Registrar and other staff

• The Council shall-

• Appoint a fit and proper person who shall be a member of the Council to be the Registrar for purpose of this bill; and;

• Such other persons as the Council may, from time to time, deem necessary to assist the Registrar in the performance of his functions under this bill.

(2) A person shall be qualified to be appointed to the office of the Registrar of the Council if he-

(a) Is a citizen of Nigeria;

(b) A Registered Facility Manager by the Council and member of;

(c) Nigeria Facility Management Association (NFMA)

(3) The Registrar shall prepare and maintain, in accordance with rules made by the Council, a register of names, addresses and approved qualifications and of such particulars, as may be specified in the rules, of all persons who are entitled, in accordance with the provisions of this bill.

(4) The register shall consist of details of membership in the 2 categories namely-

• Registered Facility Manager

• Corporate Member

(5) Subject to clause, the Council shall make rules with respect to necessary professional forms and keeping of the register and the necessary professional forms and keeping of register and the making of entries of particulars in-

(a) Regulating the making of application for enrolment or registration, as the case may be, and providing for the nature of evidence to be produced in support of the applications

(b) Providing for the notification to the Registrar by the person to whom any registered particulars concern, of any change in those particulars

(c) Specifying the fees, including annual subscription/practice fees, to be paid by members of the Council and:

(e) Specifying anything failing to be specified under this clause.

(6) Any rule for the purpose of sub clause (5) (d) of this clause shall not come into force until they are confirmed at a special meeting of the Council convened for the purpose or at the next Annual General Meeting, as the case may be.

(7) The Registrar shall together prove that a member of the Council was so registered at the date of the edition or the list of corrections, as the case may be and, that any person not so named should not be so registered.

(8) Where in accordance with sub-clause (2) of this clause, a person is, in any proceedings, shown to have been, or not have been registered at a particular date, lies shall, unless the contrary is proved, be taken or the purpose of those proceedings, having at all material times thereafter continued to be or not be so registered.

## Section 10

Funds of the Council

(1) There shall be established for the Council an account which shall be managed and controlled by the Council.

(2) There shall be paid into the fund established pursuant to sub-clause (1) of this clause-

(a) (i) Membership registration fees,

(ii) Annual Membership practice fees,

(iii) Induction fees,

(iv) Exposition, conferences, seminars, workshop and symposium,

(v) Training fees,

(vi) Grants,

(vii) Debenture,

(viii) Private and public facility certification fees,

(ix) Return on investment,

(x) Sales of facility management books, emblems, journals, magazines and other periodicals,

(xi) Default charges

• Such monies shall be made payable to the Council whether in the course of discharge of its functions or otherwise; and

(3) There shall be paid out of the account of the Council-

• All expenditure incurred by the Council in the discharge of its functions under this bill;

• Salaries and allowances of the Registrar and other employees of the Council; and

• Such reasonable travelling, subsistence allowances and other expenses as may be approved by the Council.

(4) Subject to guidelines issued by the Council from time to time, the Council’s funds and assets shall be invested in any bond, bill or other security issued guaranteed by the Federal Government or Central Bank of Nigeria.

(5) The Council may from time to time, borrow money for the purpose of the Council and may mortgage or charge the properties and assets of the Council or any part thereof and issue of debenture and other securities whether outright or as securities for any debt, liability or obligation of the Council and any interest or charge payable on monies so borrowed shall be paid out of the fund of the Council.

(6) The Council shall keep proper account on behalf of the Council in accounts to be audited by an auditor in accordance with the guidelines of the Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria.

(7) The auditor appointed for the purpose of this clause, shall not be a member of Council.

(8) All assets and liabilities held or incurred immediately before the commencement date, by or on behalf of the Council shall vest in the Council and be held by it for the purpose of the Council

(9) The provision of second schedule to this bill shall have effect with respect to the matters arising from transfer of assets and liabilities to the Council of the properties of the Council and with respect to other matters mentioned in that schedule.

## Section 11

Registration of Members

(1) Subject to Clause 5 of this bill, a person should be entitled to be registered as a member of the profession if he satisfies the Council that;

• Immediately before the commencement of this bill, he holds a qualification approved for members of the Council and has the prescribed post qualification experience;

• He is by law entitled to practice for all purposes as facility management practitioner in the Country in which the qualification was granted; and

• He holds at least one of the Country in which the qualifications prescribed for the purpose of registration and has complied with the other requirements prescribed under this bill.

(2) A person shall be entitled to be registered under this bill if he holds such certificate as approved by the Council.

(3) A person shall be entitled to be accredited as a member of the Council if he produces sufficient evidence to the Council that prior to the commencement of this bill he has been in practice in accordance with directions of the Council. 12 Approval of Qualifications, etc The Council may-

• Approve course of training which are intended for person seeking to become a member

• Prescribe the period of practical training and experiences of a member before such a member will be qualified for registration or issuance of license to practice as Registered Member;

• Prescribe the amount and date of payment of the annual practicing fees;

• Restrict the right to practice as member if default of payment of annual practicing fees continues for longer than such period as may be prescribed by the Council;

• Restrict the right to practice as a member if the qualification obtained outside Nigeria does not entitle the holder to practice as a Registered Member;

• Prescribe any other fees or commission payable by member or any other person;

• Accredit any Council whether in Nigeria or elsewhere, which the Council considers is properly organized and equipped for conducting the whole or any part of a program of training approved by the Council.

• Set up the Nigerian Facility Management Association (NFMA)

## Section 13

Establishment of Disciplinary Tribunal and Investigative Panel

(1) There shall be a body to be known as the Facility Management Council of Nigeria disciplinary Tribunal (in this bill referred to as “the Tribunal”) charged with the duty of considering and determining all cases referred to it by the Investigating Panel established under subclause (3) of this clause, and any other case which the Tribunal takes cognizance under this bill.

(2) The Tribunal shall consist of a Chairman and six other members who shall be appointed by the Council from among members of the Council, who are not members of the Governing Council.

(3) There shall be a body to be known as the Facility Management Council of Nigeria Investigating Panel (in this bill referred to the “Panel”) charged with the duties of;

• Conducting a preliminary investigation into cases where it is alleged that a member have committed any professional misconduct, or should for other reason committed any act which is subject of proceedings before the Tribunal; and

• Deciding whether the case shall be referred to the Tribunal after affording such a member opportunity of being heard either personally or by legal practitioner of his own choice in Nigeria.

• The Council shall appoint members of the Panel from members of the Council who are not members of the Governing Council or Tribunal.

• A person shall not be appointed as a member of Tribunal or of the Panel unless such a person is a registered member of the Council.

• The provisions set out in schedule to bill so far as they are applicable to the Tribunal and Panel; respectively have effect with respect to the bodies.

• The Council may, from time to time, make rules consistent with this bill as to acts, conducts or commissions which constitute professional misconduct.

## Section 14

Professional Misconduct

• Where-

• A person enrolled or registered under this bill is adjudged by the Tribunal to be guilty of infamous conduct in any professional respect;

• A person enrolled or registered under this bill is convicted by any court or Tribunal in Nigeria or elsewhere having power to impose a term of imprisonment for an offence (whether or not punishable with imprisonment) which in the opinion of the Tribunal is incompatible with the status of a facility management professional; or

• The Tribunal is satisfied that the name of any person has been fraudulently enrolled or registered; he shall be guilty of an offence and shall on conviction be liable to a term of imprisonment not exceeding five years.

• The Tribunal may, if it deems fit-

• Give a directive reprimanding that person or ordering the Registrar to strike his name off the relevant part of the register; or

• Deter or further defer its decision as to the giving of such directive under this clause until a subsequent meeting of the Tribunal.

• The Tribunal shall not deferred decision under this clause for periods exceeding one year in the aggregate and no person shall be a member of the Tribunal for the purposes of reaching a decision which has been deferred or further deferred unless he was absent as a member of the Tribunal when that decision was deferred.

• For purpose of sub clause (1)(b) of this clause, a person shall not be treated as convicted unless the conviction stands and when no appeal or further appeal is pending or no application for extension of time to appeal is brought in connection with the conviction,

• When the Tribunal gives a directive under sub-clause (2) of this clause, the Tribunal shall cause notice or the directive to be served on the person to whom it relates.

• The person to whom a directive given under sub-clause (2) of those clause relates may, at any time within 21 days from the date of service on him or notice of the directives, appeal against the directives at a Federal High Court and the Tribunal may appear as respondent to the appeal and for the purpose or enabling directives to be given as to the costs or the appeal of proceedings before the Federal High Court, the Tribunal shall be deemed to be party there to whether or not it appears on the hearing of the appeal.

• A directive of the Tribunal under this clause shall take effect where-

• No appeal is brought against the directive within the time limited in the appeal;

• Such an appeal is brought and withdrawn or struck out for want of prosecution on the withdrawal or striking out of appeal; and

• Such appeal brought is not withdrawn or struck out but the appeal is dismissed.

• A person whose name is struck off the register in pursuance of a directive of the Tribunal under the section shall not be entitled to be registered again except in pursuance of a direction on that behalf given by the Federal High Court on the application of that person. A directive under clause for the striking off a person’s name from the register may prohibit an application under this subsection.

• By that person until the expiration of such period from the date of the directive and where he has recently made such an application as may be specified in the directive.

• The Tribunal shall liaise with relevant law enforcement agencies to apprehend perpetual defaulters.

## Section 15

Miscellaneous

(1) If any person for the purpose of procuring the registration of any name, qualification or other matter-

• Makes a statement which he or she knows to be false in a material particular; or

• Recklessly makes a statement which is false in a material time particular, he or she is guilty of an offence.

(2) If, on or after the commencement date of this bill, a person who is not a member of the Council practices or holds himself out as a member in expectation of a reward or takes or uses any name, title addition or description implying that he is member, he is guilty of an offence.

(3) If the Registrar or any other person employed by or on behalf of the Council willfully makes any falsification in any matter relating to the register, he is guilty of an offence.

(4) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable;

• On summary conviction to a fine not exceeding N50,000.00; and

• On conviction or indictment to a fine not exceeding N20,000.00 Or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or both such fines and imprisonment.

(5) Where an offence under (this clause has been committed by a body corporate is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of or attributable to any neglect on the part of any Director, Manager, Secretary, or other similar officer of the corporate body or any person purporting to act in such capacity, he or she as well as the body corporate shall be deemed to have committed the offence and shall be liable to be prosecuted and punished accordingly:

## Section 16

Gazetting Power to Make Rules

• Any regulation made under this bill shall be published in the Federal Government Gazette as soon as they are made and copy of the regulations shall be forwarded to the Ministry of the Environment and office of the Head of Service not less than seven days before they are published.

• The rules proposed for the purpose of this bill shall be subject to confirmation by the Council at its next general meeting or at any special meeting of the Council convened for the purpose and if then annulled shall case to have effect on the day after the date of annulment, but without prejudice to anything done in pursuance to intended profession of such rule.

## Section 17

Powers of the Governing Council to Make Rules

(1) The Council may make rules:

• For the training of registered members of the profession or suitable persons in facility management methods

• For the supervision, regulation, engagement and training of such persons;

• Prescribing the amount and date for payment of the annual subscription, and for such purpose, different amounts may be prescribed by the rules according to whether member of the Council is Registered Member, or Corporate Member;

• Prescribing the form of license to practice to be issued annually or the Council deems fits by endorsement on an existing license;

• Restricting the right to practice as a member of the annual subscription where the default continues for longer than such period as may be prescribed by the rules;

• Restricting the right to practice as a member of the profession if the qualification granted outside Nigeria does not entitle the holder in practice to be completed before a person qualifies for a license to practice as a member of the profession; and

• Prescribing the period of practical training in the office of a member of the profession in a practice to be completed before a person qualifies for a license to practice as a member of the profession

(2) The rules, when made, shall be published in the federal government Gazette.

## Section 18

Provision of Library Facility The Council shall:

• Provide and maintain a library comprising books and publications for the advancement of knowledge of facility services and facility management and such books and publications as the Council may think necessary for the purpose;

• Support and encourage the establishment of training centres or departments for the training of facility management in Polytechnics & Universities

• Encourage advanced academic research into facility management, building services, environments, sustainability, oil and gas facilities, health care facilities, and energy and power methodology such allied subjects to extent that the Council may, from time to time, consider necessary; and

• Publish books, journals, magazines, other periodicals and organize expositions.

## Section 19

Interpretation and Citation In this bill; “Council” means the Council established as the governing body of the Council under clause 2(1) of this bill; “Corporate member” means an employer of labour that qualifies under section 5(1)(f) of this bill; “Enrolled” means in relation to registered members, “Fees” includes annual subscriptions; “Council” means the Facility Management Council of Nigeria established under clause 1(1) of this bill “Investigating Panel” means Facility Management Council of Nigeria Investigating Panel established under clause 12 of this bill; “Member” means a registered member of the Council “Facility service or facility management professional” means any qualified member or person registered who is into practice or employed by any organization, MDAs, corporation and engaged in facility services, facility care, facility management, facility maintenance, facility engineering or re-engineering, facility planning or facility security management under this bill; “Facility management” denotes the general coordination of activities that are involved in facility service, facility care, facility maintenance management, facility planning and “Tribunal” means the chartered Council of facility management disciplinary Tribunal established under clause 12 of this bill. This bill may be cited as the Facility Management Council of Nigeria bill 2015.

3\. Proceeding of the Council

• Subject to the provisions of this bill, the Council may in the name of the Council make standing orders regulating the proceedings of the Council or any of their committees.

• Standing order shall provide for decision to be taken by a majority of the members and in the event of equality of vote, the President of the Council shall have a second or casting vote;

• The quorum of Council shall be 10 and the quorum of a committee of Council shall be determined by the Council. 4. Meetings of the Council

• The Council shall convene an Annual General Meeting of the Council on the 30th day of October every year or such other day as the Council may, from time to time, appoint so that if the meeting is not held within one year after previous meeting not more than 15 months shall elapse between the respective dates of two meetings.

• A special meeting of the Council may be convened by the Council at any time, not less than 20 members of the Council are informed by notice in writing addressed to the Registrar of the Council setting out the objects of the proposed meetings, the President of Council shall chair the special meeting of the Council.

• The quorum of any meeting of the Council shall be 25 members and that of any special meeting of the Council shall be 20 members.

5\. Meetings of Council

(1) Subject to the provisions of any standing order, the Council shall meet whenever it is summoned by the Chairman, and if the President is required to do so by notice in writing given to him by not less than seven other members, he shall summon a meeting of Council to be held within seven days from day on which the notice is given.

(2) At any meeting of Council, the President of the Council, who is the Chairman of the Council or in his absence, the Deputy President shall preside, but if both are absent, the members present at the meeting shall appoint one of them to preside at the meeting.

(3) Where the Council desire to obtain the advice of any person on a particular matters, the Board may co-opt him as a member for such period as the Council deems fit, but a person who is a member by virtue of this sub-paragraph, shall not be entitled to vote at any meeting of the Council and shall not count towards a quorum.

(4) Not withstanding anything in the foregoing provision of this paragraph the first meeting of the Board shall be summoned by the minister who may give such directions as he thinks fit as to procedure which shall be followed at the meeting. 6. Committees

• The Council may appoint one or more committees to carry out on behalf of the Council such functions as the Council may determine.

• A committee appointed under this paragraph shall consist of number of persons determined by the Council or whom not more than one thirds-may be person other than a member of Council shall hold office on the committee in accordance with the terms of the instrument by which he is appointed.

• A decision of a committee of the Council shall be of no effect until it is confirmed by the Council.

7\. Miscellaneous

• The fixing of the common seal of the Council shall be authenticated by the signature of the President or any other member of Council authorized generally or specially by the Council to act for that purpose.

• Any contract or instrument which, if made or executed by a person not being a body corporate would not be required to under seal, may be executed on behalf of the Council by any person generally or specially authorized to act for that purpose by the Council.

• Any document purporting to be a document duly executed under the seal of the Council shall be received in evidence and shall, unless the contrary is proved, be deemed to be executed.

• The validity of any proceeding of the Council or of a committee of Council shall not be adversely affected by any vacancy in membership, or by any defect in the appointment of a member of the Council or 15 of a person to serve on the committee or by reason that the person is not entitled to 16 do so took part in the proceedings.
