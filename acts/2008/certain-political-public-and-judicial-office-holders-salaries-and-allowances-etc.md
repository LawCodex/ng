# Certain Political, Public and Judicial Office Holders (Salaries and Allowances, etc) (Amendment) Act, 2008

/ CERTAIN POLITICAL, PUBLIC AND JUDICIAL OFFICE HOLDERS (SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES, ETC) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008

EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM

This Act amends the Certain Political, Public and Judicial Office Holders (Salaries and Allowances, etc) Act, 2002 by, among other things, increasing their annual basic salaries, allowances and fringe benefits.
